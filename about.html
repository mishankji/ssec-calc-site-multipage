About Us

Jaipur's largest e-waste management company.

We're committed to revolutionizing how the world handles Electronic and Electrical Equipment at the end of its lifecycle — blending the city's industrial heritage with a forward-thinking recovery mission.

Our Story

One circuit board at a time.

The journey of SS Envirocare began with a simple mission: to create sustainable solutions for managing electronic waste while protecting our environment for future generations. With cutting-edge processes and a passion for sustainability, we set out to redefine how the industry handles e-waste.

Today, we process 6,600 tonnes of e-waste annually and serve hundreds of satisfied clients across the country — from individual households to large corporates managing fleet-wide IT asset disposal and EPR compliance.

How it works

Five stages. One document trail.

The same sequence runs whether it's a single office cleanout or a multi-city corporate contract.

01 / 05

Schedule Pickup

Request a collection window through our team, account manager, or the online value calculator — single site or multi-location.

02 / 05

Secure Collection

Trained crews collect with specialised vehicles; every load is weighed and logged on-site to prevent data or asset loss.

03 / 05

Safe Dismantling

Devices are carefully disassembled to separate reusable components from material requiring special processing.

04 / 05

Eco-Friendly Recycling

Materials are recovered using sustainable methods, and hazardous components are handled under CPCB-compliant methods.

05 / 05

Certificate of Disposal

You receive official documentation confirming proper disposal — ready for compliance, EPR and sustainability reporting.
